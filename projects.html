Case study: Mama Mboga Shop

Grace ran a busy neighbourhood grocery and took orders by phone and WhatsApp. Orders were missed, prices went out of date and customers had no way to pay in advance.

ResearchI spent a day in the shop and interviewed ten regular customers to learn how they order.
DesignA simple catalogue with large photos, categories and a short checkout, designed in Figma first.
BuildReact front end, Node.js back end and PostgreSQL for products and orders, with M-Pesa STK push for payments.
TestingTested on three low-cost Android phones and a slow 3G connection; pages load in under three seconds.
LaunchTrained the shop team to update prices and stock; delivered a one-page guide.
OutcomeOnline orders doubled in two months and phone-order mistakes dropped sharply.
