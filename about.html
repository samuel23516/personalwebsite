About me

My story, background and goals

Who I am and how I got here.

I grew up in Nairobi, where I taught myself HTML on a borrowed laptop at the age of 15. I built a website for my school’s football club, and the thrill of seeing something I made used by real people has stayed with me ever since.

I studied Computer Science at the University of Nairobi and have worked as a junior developer and as a freelancer. Today I focus on websites and web apps for organisations that are often overlooked by big agencies: local shops, schools, churches and non-profits.

I care about accessibility and performance. Many of my clients’ visitors use older phones and slow connections, so I design with that in mind from the start.

Why I do this work

Many good businesses and organisations in Kenya still have no website, or have one that is slow, outdated or impossible to update. I enjoy solving that problem. A well-built website helps a shop reach new customers, helps a school share results with parents and helps a non-profit raise support. That practical difference is what motivates me.
